# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

PlayToday contributors must collaborate respectfully, inclusively, and professionally. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels; give specific, constructive feedback; respect boundaries and privacy; and focus disagreement on evidence and project outcomes.

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an approved private GitHub contact mechanism. Do not publish sensitive reports in an issue. No email address or legal enforcement contact is designated by this foundation repository.

Repository maintainers may clarify expectations, edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or ban repeated or severe misconduct. They should review reports impartially, protect reporter privacy where practical, avoid conflicts of interest, and document proportionate decisions.

This code applies to repositories, reviews, issues, discussions, and project-affiliated events or communications.
