## Original plan and Phase 2D state

The visible 26-step plan covered audit, mandatory skills, research, narrative, every required section, motion, metadata, responsiveness, accessibility, tests, documents and final verification. Phase 2D supplied a separate public shell, typed routes, header/drawer, footer, marketing primitives, states and placeholders. Its automated foundation was healthy; browser evidence remained unavailable.

## Skills, research and rejected patterns

Taste Skill and UI/UX Pro Max were read and actively applied. Research supported analytical hierarchy, high contrast, mobile-first adaptation and restrained feedback. The implementation rejected generic centred SaaS composition, repeated equal cards, glass, gradients, serif replacement, stock imagery, decorative motion, fake proof and bookmaker aesthetics.

## Narrative and implementation

The implemented story moves from positioning and fictional research preview through values, coverage and workflow into Target Odds, Daily Edge and AI explanation; then makes losses visible before compatibility, analytics, trust, responsibility, plans, FAQ and final CTA. Typed fictional content lives in `home-demo-data.ts`. Homepage components and one responsive CSS module remain server-rendered.

## Accessibility and responsive verification

One H1, semantic sections, labelled demonstration, native FAQ, non-colour settlement statuses, chart summary, touch targets and reduced-motion behavior are implemented. Mobile linearizes analytical grids; larger viewports progressively restore asymmetric relationships.

## Deviations, commands and status

Overlapping core ideas were combined into coherent section groups to control length. UI/UX Pro Max’s suggested glass/orange/serif direction was rejected in favor of the existing PlayToday design system. The redundant project-local skill copies introduced a format warning and were excluded after global installation was verified. Full command outcomes and browser limitations are recorded in the completion report. Final PASS depends on all required verification evidence.
