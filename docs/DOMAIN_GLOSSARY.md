# Domain Glossary

These definitions are canonical unless superseded by an accepted decision record.

| Term                          | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fixture**                   | A scheduled sporting event between known participants, associated with a competition, start time, status, and provider identifiers.                                                                                                         |
| **Competition**               | A league, cup, tournament, or other organized set of fixtures governed by common rules.                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Market**                    | A standardized question about a fixture, such as match winner, total goals, or both teams to score.                                                                                                                                         |
| **Outcome**                   | One possible result offered within a market, such as Home, Draw, Away, Over 2.5, or Yes.                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Selection**                 | An outcome chosen for analysis or inclusion in a ticket, tied to a fixture, universal market, line where applicable, odds observation, and model output.                                                                                    |
| **Ticket**                    | A permanently recorded single or multi-selection prediction artifact, including its purpose, generation context, original odds, risk profile, timestamps, model versions, and settlement state. It is not a wager accepted by the platform. |
| **Ticket leg**                | One selection within a ticket, independently tracked and settled even after the ticket’s overall result is known.                                                                                                                           |
| **Target odds**               | The user’s desired combined decimal odds. It is a construction objective, not a guaranteed exact result or quality override.                                                                                                                |
| **Implied probability**       | The simple probability represented by decimal odds, calculated as `1 / decimal odds`; bookmaker margin means it is not automatically a fair probability.                                                                                    |
| **Estimated probability**     | A model’s calibrated estimate of an outcome occurring, with version, timestamp, input scope, and uncertainty.                                                                                                                               |
| **Fair odds**                 | Decimal odds derived from an estimated probability, commonly `1 / estimated probability`, before bookmaker margin and subject to calibration limitations.                                                                                   |
| **Model confidence**          | A bounded diagnostic of how strongly a model supports an output given its evidence and uncertainty. It is not a guaranteed win probability and must not be displayed as one.                                                                |
| **Data Quality Score**        | A documented score reflecting completeness, freshness, consistency, provider reliability, sample adequacy, and other input-quality checks. It does not measure the chance of a selection winning.                                           |
| **Daily Edge**                | The platform’s official, timestamped daily selection or ticket, published only when qualification rules pass and retained permanently after publication.                                                                                    |
| **Rollover cycle**            | A sequence of rollover days tracked against declared rules and a starting reference amount in simulation or user-recorded tracking. The platform does not custody funds.                                                                    |
| **Rollover day**              | One scheduled decision point in a rollover cycle, containing a published selection/ticket or a pass-day record.                                                                                                                             |
| **Ticket-cutting selection**  | The first definitively lost leg that makes a multi-selection ticket incapable of winning under its settlement rules. All remaining legs continue to settle.                                                                                 |
| **Settlement**                | The deterministic evaluation of a selection or ticket against verified official results and versioned market rules.                                                                                                                         |
| **Void**                      | A settlement result in which the selection contributes neutral odds according to the applicable market rules, commonly decimal factor 1.00; exact behavior must be rule-versioned.                                                          |
| **Postponed**                 | An event status indicating the fixture will not start at its scheduled time and may be rescheduled; settlement depends on the provider and versioned market time-window rules.                                                              |
| **Bookmaker adapter**         | A component that maps universal fixtures, markets, outcomes, lines, and statuses into a bookmaker-specific representation without owning prediction logic.                                                                                  |
| **Universal market**          | The platform’s provider-neutral canonical definition of a sports market and its settlement semantics.                                                                                                                                       |
| **Booking code**              | A short identifier associated with a set of selections. The term alone is ambiguous and must be qualified as internal or official in the interface.                                                                                         |
| **Official booking code**     | A code created or validated through an authorized bookmaker integration and usable on that bookmaker under its rules and expiry. It must not be claimed without approved access.                                                            |
| **Bookmaker-ready selection** | A mapped list of fixtures, markets, outcomes, and observed odds formatted to help a user identify selections on a named bookmaker. It is not an official code and does not guarantee current availability.                                  |
| **Pass day**                  | A dated, immutable record that no Daily Edge candidate met qualification thresholds. It is a valid outcome, not missing data.                                                                                                               |
| **Simulation mode**           | A clearly labeled, non-monetary environment for recording hypothetical tickets or rollover cycles. It accepts no stakes and produces no withdrawable value.                                                                                 |

## Status vocabulary

Selection statuses: Draft, Published, Not started, Live, Currently winning, Currently losing, Won, Lost, Void, Postponed, Suspended, Cancelled, and Partially settled.

Ticket statuses: Pending, In progress, Won, Lost, Void, Partially settled, and Cancelled.

Display wording may be localized, but stored meanings and permitted transitions must remain controlled and versioned.
